# Version 1.0.0 (Build 3)
**Status:** Internal Testing

**Release Date:** June 2026

## Added

### Core Application
- Initial Expo React Native application.
- Bottom tab navigation.
- Home screen.
- Breaking News screen.
- Latest News screen.
- Sports screen.
- Watch screen.
- Search screen.
- Saved Stories screen.
- Article reader.

### WordPress Integration
- Live WordPress REST API integration.
- Home feed populated from WordPress.
- News category integration.
- Sports category integration.
- Breaking News category integration.
- Forest County News integration.
- Live Video category integration.
- Top Story controlled by a dedicated WordPress tag.

### Reading Experience
- Full article display.
- Featured images.
- Related stories.
- Relative timestamps throughout the application.
- Pull-to-refresh support.
- Infinite scrolling.
- Pagination.

### Search & Saved Stories
- Search across WordPress content.
- Save stories for later reading.
- Remove saved stories.
- Saved story persistence using AsyncStorage.

### Video
- Watch tab.
- YouTube video embedding.

### Notifications
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) integration.
- Push notifications.
- Notification preferences.
- Breaking News notifications.
- Public Safety notifications.
- Weather Emergency notifications.
- Live Video notifications.
- Preferences saved locally.

### Settings
- Settings screen.
- Privacy Policy link.
- Contact Us link.
- Send Feedback link.
- Version and build information.

### Advertising
- Rotating sponsorship framework.
- In-feed advertising placements.
- Home page sponsorship support.

### Branding
- Your Daily Local branding.
- Custom splash screen.
- Adaptive app icon.
- Google Play feature graphic.
- Play Store screenshots.

### Google Play
- Google Play Console configured.
- Internal Testing track configured.
- Data Safety declaration completed.
- Content Rating completed.
- Privacy Policy published.
- Initial Android App Bundle (.aab) released.
